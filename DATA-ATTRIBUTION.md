# Data attribution

The MIT license in `LICENSE` covers the code in this repository only. It does
not cover the data.

Parking status data comes from Taipei City Government open data: the TCMSV
road-parking status feed (臺北市路邊停車即時格位資料), provided by Taipei City
Government (臺北市政府).

That data is used under the Open Government Data License, version 1.0
(政府資料開放授權條款－第1版, OGDL-Taiwan-1.0):
https://data.gov.tw/license

Published shards are derived from that data and remain subject to the same
license and attribution requirements.
